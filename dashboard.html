🔑 Key Findings from Literature (Fase 0 Relevant)

📌 BTC Volatility is Genuinely Rough — Hurst exponent H ≈ 0.01–0.06, calibrated on 30 Deribit surfaces (Caruso 2026). Far rougher than equities (H≈0.1). rBergomi with Hybrid+Mixed scheme: RMSE 22.83pp vs 41.76pp for Cholesky+Euler baseline.
📌 GARCH: Student-t beats Normal — BTC return kurtosis >> 3. Student-t (ν≈3–6) or NIG distribution fits significantly better. Persistence α+β ≈ 0.95–0.99 (near-integrated).
📌 Mean Reversion at 15-min — 90% of 183 Binance pairs show significant directional reversal OOS (Kitron & Wengrowicz 2026). Signal in SIGN, not magnitude. Lag-1 autocorrelation ≈ 0, but "bet against previous candle" captures most alpha.
⚠️ ML Forecast Horizon Problem — Baquero 2026 survey: almost no model beats naive "today's price" baseline at 1–6 month horizon. Short horizons (1–4 hours) more tractable. ALWAYS walk-forward validate.
⚠️ Transaction Costs Kill Alpha — ML strategies at 5–10 bps costs often lose all edge. Test all strategies at realistic friction (5 bps spread for BTC spot, higher for options).
✅ Whalley-Wilmott wins real BTC hedging — Deep hedging (LSTM+CVaR) does NOT outperform classical friction-aware methods on real Deribit data. Whalley-Wilmott no-trade band: saves $1.79/episode, trades 8x less (Kumar 2026).
